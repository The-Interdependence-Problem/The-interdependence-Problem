We would like to acknowledge, that all agents and living entities living on planet Earth, live in a state of interdependence. This is a consequence of the principle that all future states are conditioned
on past actions and their consequences. The actions of all agents will influence everything, to some extent. 



## Why Care?

Why should we acknoledge this? Is this a trivial fact? Or a fact woven into experience, and all experience, recursively, all the way down, up, right, left, top, bottom and to the side. It is everpresent,
everywhere and everywhen. Why care about this, if we can decide, not to care about it? How can we distinguish how much to care about? Is it possible to do so a priori? Has humanity learned lessons to make it necessary to learn the lesson of Acknowledging interdepdence?
